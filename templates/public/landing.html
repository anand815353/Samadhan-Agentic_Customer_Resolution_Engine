Agentic customer resolution for mock digital lending support

Samadhan is a GCP-ready, agentic customer resolution engine for digital lending support. It combines LangGraph-controlled workflows, policy RAG, mock internal tools, guardrails, risk-aware ticketing, human escalation, audit logs, and evaluation — all on fake seeded data.

The problem

Digital lending customer support spans loan application status, KYC and document issues, EMI and payment disputes, refund or reversal questions, NOC and closure certificates, bureau reporting complaints, fraud and security alerts, relationship-manager callbacks, and top-up eligibility queries. Manual triage is slow and inconsistent.

Samadhan demonstrates how a controlled agentic workflow can resolve or safely route these issues with deterministic guardrails, policy-backed answers, mock tool lookups, and human escalation when risk or sensitivity requires it.

Ten seeded customer personas cover realistic fintech support flows. All data is fake and for demo only.

    Check mock KYC status and suggest next steps.

Key capabilities

  • LangGraph workflow

    Controlled agentic orchestration with deterministic guardrails.

  • Policy RAG

    Retrieve approved policy and SOP knowledge via Qdrant for safe answers.

  • Mock fintech tools

    Loan status, KYC, repayments, transactions, offers — demo data only.

  • Risk-aware ticketing

    Tier 2 conversations, Tier 1 service requests, and Human Review queues.

  • Human escalation

    High-risk cases route to support-agent review — never auto-closed.

  • Audit logs

    Deterministic tool and decision logs with sensitive data masked.

  • Golden evaluation & LLMOps

    Regression cases for intent, routing, guardrails, and response safety.

Architecture highlights

  • FastAPI modular monolith
  • Jinja + HTMX + Tailwind + Alpine
  • MongoDB operational data
  • Qdrant policy RAG · Redis local-first
  • Role-based access: customer, support agent, admin
  • Optional LangSmith tracing · Cloud Run-ready

Mock demo boundaries

  • All customers, loans, applications, and transactions are fake seeded demo data.
  • No real banking, lending, payment, refund, KYC, bureau, or account-freeze operations are performed.
  • Mock tools may simulate lookups and create review requests or tickets — they never move real money or change real accounts.
