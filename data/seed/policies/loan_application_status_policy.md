# Loan Application Status Policy (Demo)

> Mock demo policy for Samadhan. Not a real lending policy.

## Purpose

Guide support agents on communicating loan application status to customers.

## Key points

- Applications move through stages: submitted, KYC review, underwriting review, approved, rejected, or pending.
- When KYC is pending, ask the customer to upload or re-upload required documents.
- Share only the current application stage and next customer action.
- Do not promise approval timelines beyond general guidance.
- Escalate to Human Review if the customer disputes stage information after tool verification.
