# RM Callback SOP (Demo)

> Mock demo SOP for Samadhan. Not a real relationship management policy.

## Purpose

Handle relationship manager assignment and callback requests.

## Key points

- Fetch assigned RM details from internal mapping records when available.
- Share RM name, branch, and callback availability in customer-safe language.
- Create a callback request record when the customer asks to speak with their RM.
- Do not share personal contact details beyond approved demo fields.
- Confirm that a callback request has been logged for follow-up.
