# EMI Payment Dispute SOP (Demo)

> Mock demo SOP for Samadhan. Not a real collections policy.

## Purpose

Handle duplicate or disputed EMI debit inquiries.

## Key points

- Verify EMI debit transactions using internal mock transaction records.
- If a duplicate debit candidate exists, acknowledge the concern and open a review.
- Do not promise automatic refund completion in the demo.
- Explain typical reconciliation timelines in general terms only.
- Escalate duplicate EMI cases to Human Review; do not auto-close.
