# NOC and Loan Statement SOP (Demo)

> Mock demo SOP for Samadhan. Not a real document operations policy.

## Purpose

Guide requests for No Objection Certificates and loan statements.

## Key points

- Check internal loan closure and NOC status before responding.
- If NOC is pending, share the current status and expected next step.
- Loan statements can be generated from repayment and transaction history in the demo.
- Do not share internal batch or system job identifiers with customers.
- Offer document generation when internal records show the loan is closed or active as requested.
