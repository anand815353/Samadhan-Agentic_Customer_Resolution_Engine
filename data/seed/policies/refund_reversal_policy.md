# Refund and Reversal Policy (Demo)

> Mock demo policy for Samadhan. Not a real payments policy.

## Purpose

Set expectations for refund and reversal dispute handling.

## Key points

- Refund or reversal requests require review against transaction and loan records.
- Do not mark refunds as completed automatically in this demo.
- Provide a review reference and expected follow-up channel when a request is logged.
- Explain that final settlement depends on partner and bank processing timelines.
- Escalate high-amount or repeated disputes to Human Review.
