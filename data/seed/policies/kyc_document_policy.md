# KYC Document Policy (Demo)

> Mock demo policy for Samadhan. Not a real KYC policy.

## Purpose

Guide customers on acceptable KYC documents and reupload steps.

## Key points

- Acceptable documents include government ID, address proof, and bank statements as listed in the application checklist.
- If a document is unclear or expired, request a reupload with specific format guidance.
- Do not claim real-time verification with external agencies in this demo.
- Mask document identifiers in chat and audit views.
- Escalate repeated KYC failures to Human Review.
