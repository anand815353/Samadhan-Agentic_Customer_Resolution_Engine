# Rejection Reason Communication Policy (Demo)

> Mock demo policy for Samadhan. Not a real lending policy.

## Purpose

Ensure rejection explanations remain customer-safe and non-discriminatory.

## Key points

- Use only approved, customer-safe rejection reasons from application records.
- Do not disclose internal risk scores, model probabilities, or underwriting cutoffs.
- Explain that the decision is based on eligibility criteria at the time of review.
- Offer general guidance on reapplying after improving eligibility, without guarantees.
- Route disputes about rejection wording to Human Review when needed.
