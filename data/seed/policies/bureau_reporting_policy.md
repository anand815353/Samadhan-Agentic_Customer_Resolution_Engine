# Bureau and CIBIL Reporting Policy (Demo)

> Mock demo policy for Samadhan. Not a real bureau reporting policy.

## Purpose

Explain bureau update timelines after loan closure or status changes.

## Key points

- Internal loan status may update before credit bureau records reflect the change.
- Bureau reporting batches are submitted on a periodic schedule in this demo.
- Typical customer-visible bureau updates may take around 30 to 45 days after submission.
- If internal records show closed but bureau still shows active, explain reporting lag calmly.
- Offer a closure letter or reference document when the loan is closed internally.
